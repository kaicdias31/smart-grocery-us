# Smart Grocery US

A fitness nutrition, healthy recipe, meal planning and workout web app focused on the United States.

## Product direction
- English only (en-US)
- USD pricing
- US customary measurements
- Black + neon green fitness/technology UI
- 12 free recipes plus individually unlockable paid recipes
- Recipe bundles with credits
- Pro plan for My Day personalization and workout features
- Paid recipes are separate from Pro

## Development
This repository is the clean rebuild of Smart Grocery. Keep the app modular and avoid changing framework/build configuration unless necessary.
